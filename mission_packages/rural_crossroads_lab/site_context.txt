RURAL CROSSROADS LAB — SYNTHETIC SIMULATION ONLY

Purpose: demonstrate whether GPT L4 and L3 generalize from the Llombai
airfield reconnaissance image to a visually distinct rural environment.

This is a fully invented aerial-style illustration converted to georeferenced
GeoTIFF (EPSG:3857) for repeatable OFFLINE visual reasoning. Its positions
and WGS84 conversions do NOT correspond to a real operating site and are
NOT authorized destinations for the physical rover.

Mission intent: Reconnoitre the area. Identify visible features worth
observing, and propose observation positions based on the image.

Possible visual hypotheses (not mandatory preassigned goals):
unpaved roads or tracks and their junction; small farm-like structures
with a yard; orchard or patterned rows; tree patches; water-filled area.
Do not assume any feature can be traversed or observed from a proposed
pixel. The map has no elevation, permissions, entrances, obstacle heights
or ground truth about traversability.

L4 should derive up to four visually supported observation objectives.
L3 should select one plausible image pixel per objective, then the
deterministic georeferencing routine converts pixels to WGS84.

The resulting L3 GPS values must NEVER be passed to powered rover control.
For field work, replace this entire package with a real, verified map and
operator-validated field constraints.
