## Detailed observations

### 1. Firmware / ultrasonic protocol

The RA4M1 responds `PONG`, and the running trace repeatedly shows `DISTANCE_CM=-1.0 BLOCKED=0 SENSOR_STATE=CLEAR`, as intended by the experimental firmware policy. The system now preserves `front_distance_valid=False` for diagnostics without using it alone as an L2 movement veto. Valid readings also occur (e.g. 80.4, 58.1, 50.1 cm). There were **no** `BLOCKED=1` messages. This test does **not** validate real close-range stop behavior: a controlled obstacle test is still needed.

### 2. Vision as the dominant reason for HOLD

Early cycles 2–3 show `obstacle_ahead=True`, `free_direction=none`, `HOLD` even though the ultrasonic subsystem is nonblocking. At cycles 29–30, vision reports a front obstacle at about 0.7–0.8 m and a right-side obstacle around 0.6 m; it also suggests the left as the free direction, yet the deterministic planner remains in `HOLD`. **Do not automatically override these HOLDs:** turning with a nontraversable forward corridor requires validated swept-path clearance.

### 3. Recovery reverse / unobserved rear space

Nine automatic reverses were commanded after persistent visual obstruction (e.g. cycle 4, 8, 13, 31). This indicates recovery triggers are active and recurrent. The forward camera / forward rangefinder do not prove that the rearward area is clear. Before another unsupervised motorized run, review this behavior, ideally disabling autonomous reverse unless rear clearance is independently confirmed.

### 4. Navigation/GPS and progress

At initialization the optional USB u-blox receiver path is missing, but later phone position and attitude are supplied. Cycle 1 stops for absent valid phone GPS; there are also STOP actions in cycles 55 and 68, whose exact underlying causes should be checked before attributing all three to GPS. GPS reported accuracy fluctuates substantially (examples in the log include ~3–21 m); the target distance remains close to 60–64 m for much of the visible run. This is insufficient evidence of reliable waypoint convergence or accurate traveled distance.

### 5. VisionNN uncertainty and diagnostic anomaly

VisionNN lines often have `accepted:false` while producing geometry/region values and tactical perception confidence of 0.7–0.9. The meaning of `accepted` should be checked against its fusion implementation; do not equate the geometry-confidence tag with acceptance of all results. Some visual snapshots have high inferred traversability and others immediately low inferred traversability; images are needed to judge whether this reflects terrain changes, pitch/camera geometry, or segmentation behavior.

### 6. Scheduling/latency

Each tactical command is held for 3 seconds followed by an `Inter-cycle STOP`; image acquisition and VisionNN inference occur between commands. The system therefore has an intentional stop-and-go behavior separate from `HOLD` decisions. The initial model startup is substantially longer than warm-cycle processing. Do not infer actual ground speed from motor command duty cycle without wheel odometry or external track measurements.
